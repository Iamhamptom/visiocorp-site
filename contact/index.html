Complaints

If something is wrong

Tell us first, at david@visiocorp.co or +27 71 548 6226. We acknowledge within two business days and give a written outcome within ten.

Contacting us does not limit any right you have. If you are a consumer and we cannot resolve the matter between us, you may refer it to the National Consumer Commission. If your complaint is about personal information, you may take it to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
