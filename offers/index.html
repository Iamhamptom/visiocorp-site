You buy the lines you want for a record. Each line is a service we perform and report on. The prices below are indicative; the exact figure is confirmed on the written quote before you pay.

How you buy

The order path, end to end

  1. Enquiry. You email us, or reach us through our social accounts, or we approach you.
  2. Written quote or agreement. We send a quote or service agreement naming the exact scope, the price, the currency, the delivery date and the revision rounds. Our Terms and Conditions and our Refund and Cancellation Policy are attached to it.
  3. Payment request. Once you accept, we send a Payfast or Yoco payment request for that exact amount. The payment page shows the amount, the currency and what it is for.
  4. Delivery. We deliver the files, the booked session or the campaign as scoped.
  5. Aftercare. Revisions, reporting and support run as set out in the agreement. Complaints go to david@visiocorp.co and are acknowledged within two business days.
Currency

Quoted in USD, charged in ZAR

We quote internationally in US dollars and charge in South African rand at the rate on the day the payment request is created. The rand amount is always shown to you before you pay.

Tax

Not registered for VAT

Touchline Agency (Pty) Ltd is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to any quote, invoice or payment request.

Delivery

3 to 10 business days

Digital files are delivered within 3 to 10 business days of the later of cleared payment and our receipt of everything we need from you, unless the agreement states another date.
