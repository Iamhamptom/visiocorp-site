Who he is

Tony Duardo is a South African songwriter and producer. His catalogue sits behind records for some of the biggest names in amapiano, afrobeats and afro-house — Uncle Waffles, TYLA, Davido, CIZA, Omah Lay, Elaine, Adekunle Gold, TxC and DJ Maphorisa among them.

The list below is the working record: 38 released records, each one held in the Touchline catalogue with the publishing on it accounted for at 100%. It is the catalogue we administer, not a highlight reel — every record we hold is on it.

What a credit means here. These are publishing credits — the writing and the production behind the record. They are not artist releases and we make no claim on the recordings. The one exception is “Young People Must Be Fine” (2024), which is Tony Duardo’s own release.

No streaming, chart or sales figures are published on this page. The catalogue holds the credit and the publishing split; anything beyond that we would have to measure before we would put a number next to it.
